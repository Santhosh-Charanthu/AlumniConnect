# AlumniConnect — Non-Technical Report

---

## What is AlumniConnect?

AlumniConnect is a web platform that connects college students with alumni from their institution. Students can discover alumni who are working professionals, book mentoring sessions with them, chat in real time, and get career guidance — all in one place.

Alumni can share their experience, create mentoring sessions (free or paid), and give back to their college community by helping the next generation of students.

---

## Who is it for?

**Students** who want to:

- Learn from people who have already walked the path they are on
- Get advice on careers, interviews, and skill-building
- Book sessions with alumni at companies they aspire to join
- Connect and chat with mentors directly

**Alumni** who want to:

- Give back to their college community
- Share their knowledge and experience
- Host mentoring sessions and optionally earn from them
- Stay connected with students from their college

---

## How does it work — Step by Step

### Getting Started

1. You visit AlumniConnect and click "Get Started"
2. You enter your college email address (only `.edu` emails are accepted — this keeps the platform trusted and college-specific)
3. A one-time verification code is sent to your email
4. You enter the code to confirm your identity
5. You fill in your profile details and upload a photo
6. You choose whether you are joining as a Student or as an Alumni
7. You are in — and taken straight to your dashboard

---

### For Students

**Your Dashboard**
When you log in, you see a summary of your activity — how many sessions you have booked, how many are coming up, and which ones you have completed.

**Explore Alumni**
You can browse alumni from your college. Each alumni card shows their name, company, job title, skills, and rating. You can filter by company, skills, or sort by rating to find the right mentor.

**AI Match**
Not sure who to talk to? Click the AI Match button on your dashboard. The platform analyses your interests and automatically surfaces the top 5 alumni whose skills align most closely with what you want to learn. Each card shows a match percentage alongside the alumni's name, company, and skills, so you can make a quick decision. You can message an alumni directly from the match results or visit their full profile.

**Alumni Profile**
Clicking on an alumni takes you to their full profile — their work experience, projects, achievements, available sessions, and reviews from other students.

**Explore Sessions**
You can browse all upcoming mentoring sessions. Each session shows the topic, date, duration, price (or free), and who is hosting it.

**Booking a Session**

- For free sessions: click Register and you are in
- For paid sessions: click Book, a secure payment window opens, you pay using UPI, card, or net banking, and your spot is confirmed instantly

**After Booking**

- You get a notification inviting you to join the session's group chat
- When the session goes live, you get another notification with a link to join the meeting
- After the session ends, you can leave a star rating and written review

**Cancelling a Booking**
If you need to cancel, you can do so up to 1 hour before the session starts. If you paid, your money is refunded automatically.

**Messages**
You can send direct messages to any alumni you have connected with. You can also participate in group chats that are automatically created for each session you join.

**Notifications**
You receive notifications when:

- A new session is created by an alumni from your college
- Your session is about to go live
- A session you booked has ended (and you can now review it)
- A session you booked gets cancelled (with refund if applicable)
- You are invited to join a session group chat

---

### For Alumni

**Your Dashboard**
You see an overview of your sessions — scheduled, live, and completed — along with how many students have registered.

**Your Profile**
You can build a rich profile with:

- Your current company and job title
- An "About" section
- Work experience history
- Projects you have built
- Achievements and certifications
- Your hourly rate and availability

**Creating a Session**
You fill in a title, description, category, date and time, duration, registration deadline, and price (or set it to free). You upload a cover image. Once created, a group chat for the session is automatically set up, and all students from your college are notified.

**Managing Sessions**
From your sessions list you can:

- Edit session details
- View who has registered (with their department, batch year, and payment status)
- Start the session when the time comes (you provide a meeting link)
- End the session when it is done

**Starting a Session**
When you are ready to begin, you click Start and paste in your Google Meet (or any video call) link. Registered students are immediately notified that the session is live.

**Ending a Session**
Once the session has run for at least half its scheduled duration, you can mark it as complete. Students are then notified and prompted to leave a review.

**Cancelling a Session**
If you need to cancel, all registered students are notified and any paid students are automatically refunded.

**Messages**
You can send and receive direct messages from students. You are also the admin of the group chat created for each of your sessions.

**Notifications**
You receive notifications when:

- A student registers for one of your sessions
- A student unregisters from one of your sessions

## Key Features at a Glance

### Verified College Community

Only people with a `.edu` college email can sign up. This ensures the platform stays within the college ecosystem and maintains trust between students and alumni.

### Mentoring Sessions

Alumni create sessions on topics they know well — interview prep, career advice, technical skills, industry insights. Sessions can be free or paid, and can accommodate multiple students at once.

### Secure Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay, a trusted Indian payment gateway. Students can pay via UPI, credit/debit card, or net banking. The platform never stores card details. Refunds are processed automatically when eligible.

### Real-Time Chat

Every session gets its own group chat the moment it is created. Students are added to the group when they register. Everyone can send text messages, images, videos, and PDF documents. Messages show read receipts and typing indicators.

Students and alumni can also message each other one-on-one through direct messages.

### Live Notifications

Important events are delivered as instant notifications — both inside the app and by email. You never miss a session going live, a new session from your college, or a booking update.

### Reviews & Ratings

After a session ends, students can rate it from 1 to 5 stars and leave a written comment. These reviews are visible on the alumni's public profile and help other students make informed decisions.

### AI-Powered Alumni Matching

When you are not sure where to start, the AI Match feature does the thinking for you. Click the "AI Match" button on your dashboard and the platform analyses your listed interests and compares them against the skills of every alumni on the platform. It returns the top 5 alumni who are the closest match — ranked by how well their expertise lines up with what you want to learn. Each result shows a match percentage so you can see at a glance how relevant each alumni is to you.

### Alumni Discovery

Students can search for alumni by name, company, job title, or skills. By default, the platform shows alumni from the same college, making the network feel personal and relevant.

## Refund Policy Summary

| Situation                                       | Outcome                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Student cancels more than 1 hour before session | Full refund                      |
| Student cancels within 1 hour of session        | Not allowed                      |
| Alumni cancels the session                      | Full refund to all paid students |

Refunds are processed through Razorpay and typically appear within 5–7 working days.

---

## Privacy & Trust

- Passwords are never stored in plain text
- Payment details are never stored on the platform — Razorpay handles all of that
- Only people with verified college emails can access the platform
- Each user can only see notifications and data relevant to their own account and role
